Who We Are

About Shanthi Constructions

Home / About Us
Established 2005 · Trichy, Tamil Nadu

Two Decades of Building Trust, Quality & Excellence

Shanthi Constructions was founded in 2005 with a singular purpose — to bring world-class construction quality to the homes and businesses of Trichy. What started as a small residential contracting firm has grown into one of Tamil Nadu's most trusted full-service construction companies.

We have successfully delivered over 650 projects — from bespoke luxury villas to large-scale commercial complexes — each one a testament to our unwavering commitment to quality, transparency, and client satisfaction.

Quality First
Premium materials and rigorous quality checks at every stage of construction.
Client-Centric
Your vision drives every decision — we listen, plan, and deliver to your expectations.
Safety Standards
Rigorous on-site safety protocols protecting our workers and your investment.
Sustainability
Eco-conscious construction practices for a greener tomorrow.
